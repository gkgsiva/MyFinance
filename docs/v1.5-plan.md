# MyFinance v1.5 plan

Built on top of v1.4. Everything new is stored in its own database file, `myfinance-v15.db`, like
v1.1 to v1.3 did, so upgrading never touches existing tables and nothing clashes with v1.4's changes.
The new file is included in encrypted backups and restores. Everything stays on the phone.

## 1. Ask in plain words
Plan > Ask (also a home screen shortcut). Type a question such as "how much on Swiggy last year?",
"food spend this month", "biggest payment in March", "salary in 2025", "how many times Uber last 3 months".
Answered on the phone by a rule-based reader (`core/Ask.kt`), no internet or AI service:
- what: spend / income / count / average / biggest / smallest
- who: matched against your payees, categories and labels (spelling-tolerant)
- when: today, yesterday, this/last week, month, year, financial year, a month name, a year, "last N days/months"
Shows the answer, how it was read ("Spend at Swiggy, 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025"), and the matching transactions.

## 2. Tax-time pack
Plan > Tax-time pack. One financial year at a time:
- Deductions by section (from v1.2's tax tags, capped at limits)
- Interest income: savings interest (80TTA) and FD/RD interest, found in credits
- Capital gains: sales you enter (buy/sell date and value, equity or other), split into short and long term
- Salary received and total income in the year
- Export as Excel (one sheet per part) to share with your CA or use for ITR filing

## 3. Insurance and warranty vault
Plan > Vault. Policies (health, life, term, motor, home, travel, other) with insurer, policy number,
cover, premium, renewal date, people covered and photos. Warranties with item, shop, purchase date,
price, warranty months and bill photos. Reminders 30, 7 and 1 day before a renewal or warranty end.

## 4. Subscription audit
Plan > Subscriptions. Built from the recurring payments the app already finds, plus known services
(Netflix, Prime, Hotstar, Spotify, YouTube, Google One, iCloud, gym...). Flags:
- Price hike: latest charge higher than before, with the old and new amount
- Duplicate: two services of the same kind (two music or video apps), or one service charged on two accounts
- Not used: you mark a subscription as not used, and it shows the yearly cost you would save
- Yearly cost of each, and the total

## 5. Cash flow calendar
Plan > Calendar. A month grid: expected salary, EMIs and auto debits, SIPs, bill reminders,
subscriptions, insurance renewals and lending due dates on their days, with past days showing what
actually happened. Tap a day for its list. Month total of money expected in and out.

## 6. Goal-linked savings
In Goals: link a SIP, FD, holding or a label (e.g. "Vacation fund") to a goal. Linked savings add to
the goal's progress automatically. Each goal shows the monthly amount going into it and the month it
will be reached at that pace (with an optional expected return).

## 7. Lending tracker
Plan > Lent & borrowed. Money lent to or borrowed from someone, with date, due date and note, and
part repayments. Shows who owes you and whom you owe, overdue entries, and a reminder on the due
date. "Remind on WhatsApp" opens a polite message to send.

## 8. Encrypted auto-backup
Manage > Backup > Auto-backup. Pick a backup file once (on the phone or in Google Drive through the
file picker) and a schedule (daily or weekly). The app writes an encrypted backup there on schedule,
using the same AES-256 encryption as manual backups. The password is kept in the Android keystore so
the backup can run on its own. Shows the last automatic backup and any failure.

## 9. Home screen shortcuts
Long-press the app icon: Quick add, Ask, Calendar. A new widget showing today's spend and the next
EMI or bill due, with a quick add button.
